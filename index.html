What we fix

Nearly everything on a car or light truck, under one roof. Not sure what's wrong? Start with a diagnosis and we'll explain what we find before any work begins.

Ask about a repair

  • Diagnostics

    Check engine lights, electrical faults, no-start problems, noises and vibrations, TPMS warnings, and pre-purchase inspections.

  • Engine & transmission

    Engine repair and replacement, transmission repair and leak inspection, driveability problems, and machine shop work.

  • Brakes & suspension

    Brake repair, steering and suspension repair, and wheel alignment.

  • Tires & wheels

    New and used tires, fitted on site.

  • Electrical

    Batteries, charging and electrical systems, onboard electronics, and lights.

  • Heating & cooling

    Air conditioning service and radiator repair.

  • Exhaust & emissions

    Exhaust repair and emissions testing.

  • Maintenance

    Oil change and lube, fluids and filters, and routine scheduled maintenance.

  • Body & glass

    Auto body repair, plus window and mirror repair and replacement.

A family shop that knows your car by name

Lucas Auto Services is an independent repair shop, not a chain. When you call, you talk to the people who will work on your car: Lucas, his son Danny, and a small team of technicians who have been fixing cars in New Jersey for more than two decades.

We tell you what's wrong, what it will take to fix, and what can wait. Then we do the work right here in our shop.
